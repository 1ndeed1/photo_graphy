About

I started photographing trips with a borrowed camera and never really stopped.

Over the last eight years I've worked on weddings, portraits and travel assignments across Kerala. I prefer natural light, quiet moments and simple edits.

I like working with people who are a bit camera-shy. A good session should feel like a relaxed afternoon, not a photoshoot.
